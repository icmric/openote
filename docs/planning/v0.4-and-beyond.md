# Openote v0.4 and beyond — the product backlog

> **Document status:** Draft v0.1 · Planning · 2026-08-04
> **Owner:** Eric · **Related:** [v0.3 student plan](v0.3-student-plan.md) (current release) · [2026-08 code review](../reviews/2026-08-code-review-v02-merge.md) (where these came from) · [ROADMAP](../../ROADMAP.md)
> **Sizes:** **S** ≈ hours · **M** ≈ 1–3 days · **L** ≈ ~a week · **XL** ≈ multi-week.

This is the standing backlog of *product* ideas, as opposed to the v0.3 plan's
release scope. It exists so suggestions stop living in review documents and chat
history. Everything here is judged against the same bar the v0.3 plan set:

> **Does this help a student the week before an exam?**

Secondary audience, always: anyone switching from OneNote, who judges us on
whether the things they did daily still work.

---

## 1. Engineering debt that gates features

Listed first because two of the product items below are blocked on them.

| # | Item | Size | Why it gates something |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | **Blob de-duplication + garbage collection** | M–L | Blobs are stored **twice** (SQLite `blobs` table *and* `.onotebook/blobs/`) and nothing ever deletes either copy. A 60-slide deck at 2× render is ~120 MB per store — **~240 MB per PDF import**, before mirrors and backups copy it again. This is the fastest way for a student to fill a disk, and **audio notes (P3) would make it far worse**. De-dup is really the ADR-0006 container demotion (make `.onotebook/blobs/` the single home); GC needs its own short ADR because a blob is referenced by pages, history snapshots, *and* other devices' logs that may not have synced yet. |
| E2 | **Demote the container to `cache.onote`** | M | The remaining half of [ADR-0006 §3](../adr/ADR-0006-sync-transport-and-text-model.md). The first device's `.onote` still sits in the synced folder being re-uploaded after every save — and it is **double-clickable on the second machine**, which is the two-writers-on-one-WAL-database case the joining flow was fixed to avoid. Structural, not theoretical. |
| E3 | **Split `AppState`** | M | ~3,000 lines across 26 sections, and every feature lands in it because there is nowhere else. `notifyListeners` then offers a rebuild of everything to everyone, which is why three separate per-keystroke caches had to be added. Extract `SyncCoordinator`, `StudyState`, `TagOps` **before** v0.4 features, not after. |
| E4 | **macOS / Linux human runs**, incl. one PDF import each | S | CI builds all three; nobody has *used* two of them. The flagship depends on pdfium loading on each. |
| E5 | **The structured `{nodes:[…]}` text model** | XL | Needed for per-run styling, paragraph collapse (U7), and real-time co-editing. Driven by sync, not the editor — see ADR-0006 §4. |

## 2. Cheap and high-leverage (candidates for v0.3.x)

These are small, and each one makes an existing feature actually get used.

### P1 — Study stats and an exam countdown · **S–M** ⭐ — **SHIPPED 2026-08-04**

> "12 cards a day and you'll have cleared Discrete Maths by the exam."

The study loop is built; the *motivation* to open it is not. A per-deck line —
cards seen, due today, current streak — and an optional **exam date on a
section**, which turns into "14 days left → 12 cards/day clears this deck".

**Correction to this entry as originally written.** It claimed "everything
needed is already in `CardState`; this is a read-only surface plus one date
field." That was wrong, and it is worth recording why, because it is the kind of
estimate that looks safe until someone tries it. `CardState` holds `dueAt` —
when a card comes *back*. Nothing anywhere recorded when a card was *reviewed*,
and `dueAt` cannot be run backwards to find out: the sub-day learning steps mean
two cards due in ten minutes may have been graded on different days. A streak is
therefore not a read-only view of existing data; it needs a ledger that did not
exist. So the work grew a third piece:

- **`app/lib/study/study_stats.dart`** — a reviews-per-local-day map, plus the
  streak, activity and countdown arithmetic. Every function takes "today" as an
  argument, so the day-boundary behaviour is testable rather than dependent on
  when CI runs; `daysBetween` goes through UTC, because subtracting local
  `DateTime`s truncates a 23-hour spring-forward day and would skip a number in
  the countdown once a year.
- **Recording**, in `AppState.gradeCard`, taken *before* the cram-mode early
  return: an hour of practice the night before an exam is the most studying a
  student does all term, and a streak that ignored it would break on the one day
  it mattered.
- **The surface** — the panel's exam banner, `SEEN n of m`, the streak line and
  a fortnight activity strip; "Set exam date…" in the section's navigator menu
  and in the panel's ⋯ menu; the command-bar study badge turning brass once the
  exam is inside a week.

Two scoping decisions worth keeping: the exam date lives in **workspace
settings**, not on the node envelope, so it does not travel through the op log
onto everyone in a shared notebook (and does not touch the frozen v1 format);
and the streak counts **every notebook**, because a week's work spread across
three modules is one habit, not three broken streaks.

35 tests (24 pure, 6 state, 5 widget — `study_panel` was on the "surfaces with
no tests" list). **Still open:** page thumbnails and dark slides (P3/P4) are
untouched, and nobody has used this on a real semester's deck.

### P2 — "Share as PDF" as a first-class action · **S** *(now unblocked)*

Vector export landed 2026-08-04, so the output is finally worth sharing:
searchable, selectable, a few hundred KB instead of tens of MB. The unit students
actually share is "my notes for week 6", so surface it as a one-click **Share**
on the page and section menus rather than leaving it inside Export.

Pairs with **printing (P13 in the v0.3 plan)**, which is the same vector page
driven through the `printing` package's OS dialog — genuinely a few hours now
that `buildPagePdf` exists.

### P3 — Dark-slide mode · **S**

Students annotate at night; a white 2× slide raster in a dark room is a
flashlight. An invert/dim toggle on locked background blocks is a colour filter,
not a re-render.

### P4 — Page thumbnails for slide sections · **S–M**

A 60-slide printout is navigated by eye, but the outline panel is text-only.
Thumbnails for pages whose first block is a locked background image would make
imported decks navigable. Cheap because the render already exists as a blob.

### P5 — Positive import report · **S**

The *failure* half is surfaced (skipped sections, dropped strokes). The success
half is not: a switcher who just imported five years of notes should see "324
pages, 372 images, 64,616 strokes arrived". The parser already counts all three.
Trust in minute one is what converts a switcher.

### P6 — HTML export · **M**

The other half of OPEN-7, and the only export a phone can read losslessly.
Linkable, unlike PDF.

## 3. The two structural student features

Both are worth doing properly rather than cheaply, and both should wait for E1.

### P7 — Audio recording pinned to notes · **L–XL** ⭐

> "Record the lecture. Tap any line later and hear what was being said when you
> wrote it."

This is the Notability feature, and for lecture-goers it is arguably a bigger
draw than flashcards — it rescues the notes you took badly because you were
listening. Nobody serves it on Windows/Linux.

The recording is the easy part. The real work is:
- **timestamp ↔ block correlation** — every edit already carries `updatedAt`, so
  the mapping is close to free *if* recorded at edit granularity;
- **the blob-size story** — an hour of AAC is ~30 MB per lecture, permanently,
  which is why **E1 comes first**;
- seek-on-tap UI, and what happens when a note is edited after the recording.

Recommend: spike recording + seek in v0.4, ship pinning in v0.5.

### P8 — OCR on images · **L**

Whiteboard photos and JPEG-imported worksheets are invisible to search today.
**The integration half is already built**: PDF import stores a hidden text layer
in `content['sourceText']` and the existing brute-force search indexes it, so OCR
is purely additive — run Tesseract (via the Rust core, so it is offline and
licence-clean) over image blobs in the background and write into the same field.

The v0.3 plan says "investigate"; the PDF work quietly answered the hard question.

## 4. For the OneNote switcher specifically

### P9 — OneNote tag import · **M** *(value went up)*

Still blocked on a real tagged `.one` fixture and the type-`0x10` walker fix. But
the *value* rose sharply since it was deferred: with tags, the rollup and
flashcards all shipped, an imported notebook's To-Dos and Questions would light
up the entire study loop on day one. Worth actively asking a user for a fixture.

### P10 — Password-protected sections · **M–L**, spec first

The one OneNote feature with no Openote answer at all. Students keep journals
next to coursework. Spec before code, because the questions are real: key
derivation from a passphrase, what is encrypted at rest, and how it interacts
with the op log — which is **plaintext JSONL today**, though `Op.encryption` is
already reserved for exactly this. Schedule the spec now so the crypto gets
thought rather than improvised.

### P11 — Group/ungroup · **M**

The last piece of CANVAS-7; alignment guides shipped without it.

## 5. Deliberately not recommended yet

Recorded with reasons so they aren't re-proposed every planning round.

- **Android / tablet build.** Not "the same app on a smaller screen": the sync
  transport is a *folder*, and Android's scoped storage / SAF makes that a new
  storage backend. Porting today would also inherit E1 and E2. Wait.
- **Ink-to-math / handwriting recognition.** High student appeal, XL cost, and
  no foundation in the current recognition surface. Revisit when the study loop
  has usage evidence.
- **Real-time co-editing.** The op log makes it a transport swap *later*.
  Shared-folder group notebooks already cover the group-study case students
  actually have, and per-character merge needs E5 first.
- **A first-party sync service.** Contradicts the "point it at a folder you
  already have" position that makes self-hosting trivial. Only if users ask.

## 6. Suggested v0.4 shape

Pulling the above into one release:

1. **E1 + E2** — blob de-dup, GC, container demotion. Unglamorous, but it is the
   difference between an app a student can leave running for a semester and one
   that eats their disk.
2. **E3** — the `AppState` split, done *before* the features below land in it.
3. ~~**P1** — study stats and exam countdown~~ — **done 2026-08-04**, ahead of
   E3 rather than after it. That is a deviation from this document's own advice
   and it cost what the advice predicted: the feature added ~180 lines to
   `AppState`, in a twenty-seventh section. The extraction it argues for is
   correspondingly slightly larger, and `StudyState` is now the obvious first
   cut.
4. **P2 + printing** — share and print, now that vector export exists.
5. **P3, P4, P5** — the small ones, as filler.
6. **P7 spike** — audio recording and seek, not yet pinned.

Deferred to v0.5: audio pinning, OCR, password sections, HTML export.
