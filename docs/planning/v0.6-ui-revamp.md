## 0. The brief, and how the review was done

The report: *"It's ok at the moment, but something about it just feels a bit
off and unprofessional, I don't know what it is."* That phrasing is the
diagnosis's job — when nobody can point at the problem, the problem is almost
never one widget. It is a *system* property.

**Method.** The app was reviewed by looking at it, not only by reading it: a
widget-test harness (`app/test/ui_screenshots_test.dart`, kept — see §6)
renders the real shell with a seeded notebook to PNG, light and dark, panels
open and closed, with the real bundled fonts at a real desktop size. On top of
that, every concrete style value in `lib/ui` was counted, the theme was
audited against what Material 3 does when left alone, and every text/surface
pairing in actual use was put through the WCAG contrast formula. The style
guide was then read against all of it.

## 1. The diagnosis

One sentence: **Openote has a palette and it has pixels, and nothing operative
in between** — no token layer, no component themes — so the app is really two
unrelated design languages sharing a window, and neither of them is finished.

That is what "off but I can't say why" is. A professional UI is mostly the
relentless repetition of a small number of values; the eye reads the rhythm
without knowing it. Openote currently repeats almost nothing. The specifics,
numbered for the plan to reference:

### U1 · Two design languages in one window

`onoteTheme()` sets colors, the font, density, and one tooltip delay —
**and not a single component theme**. Consequence: every Material widget the
app reaches for renders in stock *mobile* Material 3, side by side with the
hand-rolled dense desktop panels:

- **52 `SnackBar` call sites** render as a full-window-width dark slab —
  1440px of inverse-surface bar to say "Reminder set". This is the single most
  visible clash, and it fires constantly (save confirmations, exports, tags,
  reminders).
- **`FilledButton`/`TextButton` default to M3 stadium shapes** — every
  dialog's primary action is a *pill*, while the app's own chrome is built
  from 5–8px rectangles.
- **Dialogs default to 28px corners** on a tinted M3 surface; date and time
  pickers (the planner's flagship interactions, six call sites) are the
  full mobile calendar at mobile metrics.
- **Menus render at mobile row heights** (~40px against the style guide's 36),
  and clicks produce the Material **ink ripple** — a phone gesture echo that
  desktop apps universally disable in favour of hover/pressed tints.
- Focus rings, hover states, checkbox metrics, tooltips: all stock.

The hand-rolled side compensates per widget — 16 files check
`Brightness.dark` by hand, five right-hand panels ship four different widths
(240, 240, 260, 300, 320) — so even the custom half doesn't agree with itself.

### U2 · No token layer, so nothing repeats

Counted in `lib/ui` + `lib/markdown` + `lib/canvas`:

| Dimension | In use | Style guide says |
|---|---|---|
| Font sizes | **17 distinct** (9, 9.5, 10, 10.5, 11, 11.5, 12, 12.5, 13, 13.5, 14, 15, 18, 20, 22, 26…) | a 5-step UI scale |
| Icon sizes | **11 distinct** (12–20, 32, 34) | 16 / 20 / 24 |
| Corner radii | **13 distinct** (1.5–14, 99) | 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 |
| Overlay alphas | **12 distinct** (.06–.7) | — (unspecified) |
| Letter-spacings | 5 distinct | — |

Half-point one-off sizes (10.5, 11.5, 12.5) are the tell: each was chosen
locally, none can be reasoned about globally. Two adjacent controls are
routinely 1px apart in text size and 1–2px apart in icon size for no reason a
user could name — which is precisely the subliminal wrongness reported.

### U3 · The type is too small and the hierarchy is flat

The distribution's mass sits at **10.5–12.5px**, below the guide's own 12–14
UI range. Worse than smallness: *flatness*. The only hierarchy device in use
is the 10.5px letter-spaced all-caps label, and it is used for everything —
panel titles (STUDY, PLANNER), list groups (SECTION 1, THIS WEEK, FAVOURITES),
so every surface reads identically and nothing leads the eye. Line heights are
mostly unset, so vertical rhythm varies with whatever the font metrics give.

### U4 · The default metadata colour fails WCAG AA — per the app's own math

`graphite400` (#9A968C) is the de-facto colour of every caption, hint,
subtitle and panel label — and on `paper50` it measures **2.80:1**
(2.95:1 on white). AA requires 4.5:1 for normal text; 3:1 even for large. The
style guide simultaneously assigns `graphite400` the role "tertiary text" *and*
declares AA non-negotiable — the guide is internally inconsistent, and the app
inherited the failing half. `brass500` as accent text on light is 2.38:1.
(Dark mode is mostly fine: `moon400` on `night50` is 4.97:1.) Low-contrast
fuzzy grey at 10.5px is a large share of the "unprofessional" impression on
its own, and it is also simply an accessibility bug.

### U5 · No surface model, so regions don't compose

Which background a region gets is decided per widget: the planner panel picks
`night0` in dark — the *canvas* colour — so panel and page merge into one
undifferentiated black; the navigator's two columns pick different papers; the
status bar another. Panels draw their own borders *and* the shell inserts
`VerticalDivider`s, doubling edges. Light mode wobbles between `paper50` and
`paper100` with no rule. Dark mode has no layering story at all (the guide
defines dark *tokens* but no region mapping).

### U6 · Visible defects (each small; together they read as unfinished)

Found on the screenshots, in roughly the order a new user meets them:

1. **The command bar opens as a wall of disabled grey.** ~20 formatting
   controls render disabled until a text box has focus (§7a.2-compliant, but
   the *effect* is a washed-out first impression), and the right side is a
   run of eight adjacent icon-only buttons with no grouping.
2. **The page-title band is an overlay with no reserved space.** The title is
   `Positioned` at y≈20 in the same coordinate space as blocks; any block
   above y≈80 renders *through* it, title and content striking through each
   other. Nothing prevents or repairs that placement.
3. **The tag gutter shifts the text it decorates** — its own doc comment says
   it "must not shift the text", but it is a `Row` that prepends icons, so a
   tagged bullet indents further than its untagged siblings.
   **Attempted and reverted 2026-08-05, with the reason worth keeping.** The
   obvious fix — reserve a fixed-width gutter on *every* line — was written and
   backed out, because `edit_view_metrics_test` immediately showed it makes
   things worse: the gutter exists only in the **read** renderer
   (`MarkdownView`), while the edit renderer is a bare `TextField` with no
   gutter at all. Reserving 46px in read alone widens the edit/view mismatch
   from "tagged lines jump" to "every line jumps" — the exact complaint from
   the field report. The real fix is therefore a **paired** change: the edit
   field reserves the same left inset and the markers are painted over it in a
   `Stack`. That is a change to the app's most delicate subsystem and wants its
   own pass with the metrics tests driving it, not a padding tweak at the end
   of a revamp.
4. **The planner leaks Markdown into row titles** — a task on a list line
   shows as "- Conjunction: p ∧ q", dash included; rollups show raw source.
5. **Truncation everywhere at default widths**: page titles ellipsize in the
   navigator at its default width; the status bar cuts its own labels
   ("Sync…"). The breadcrumb bar spends a 26px full-width row duplicating
   what the navigator already shows.
6. ~~**The study panel's empty state shows grey placeholder bars.**~~
   **Withdrawn on re-inspection (2026-08-05).** Those were the mono keyboard
   hints ("Ctrl+3", "== ==") rendering as tofu because the screenshot harness
   loaded Inter but not JetBrains Mono. The empty state is in fact one of the
   better ones in the app. The harness now loads every bundled face, and the
   lesson is recorded there: a font the harness lacks looks exactly like a UI
   defect, so read tofu as a harness question first.
7. **All five right panels can open simultaneously** — 1,360px of panels on a
   Row with no policy, which on a 1366px laptop leaves the canvas at 0.
8. Carried from the field report: the **print dialog** looks dated (it is the
   stock GTK/`printing` dialog — partially out of our hands, but the parts in
   ours aren't styled), and the navigator wants OneNote-style width play —
   partially delivered (resizable columns + rail); defaults and minimums are
   tighter than comfortable.

### U7 · What is *right* and must not be lost

The bones are good: the layout (nav / canvas / one contextual bar / optional
panel) matches the guide; the palette is distinctive and warm; density is
appropriate for a desktop tool; the writing voice in-product is genuinely
excellent; dark mode exists everywhere; nearly every control has a tooltip
with its shortcut. This is a revamp, not a redesign — the guide's five
principles (§1) all survive contact with this review.

## 2. Goals

The target character, in one line: **quiet precision** — the density of a
serious desktop tool (the Linear / Things class), in Openote's warm
ink-on-paper palette, with OneNote's approachability.

Measurable exit criteria for the revamp as a whole:

1. **One design language.** No stock-M3 shape/metric visible anywhere: every
   Material component the app uses is themed to the system in §4 (buttons,
   menus, dialogs, pickers, snackbars, checkboxes, focus, hover).
2. **The token audit comes back boring.** Re-running the §U2 counts yields:
   ≤6 font sizes (integer px), 3 icon sizes, 4 radii, ≤5 named alphas — all
   from `tokens.dart`, zero inline literals in `lib/ui`.
3. **Every text/surface pairing in use passes AA.** The §U4 matrix re-run
   shows nothing under 4.5:1 for text (3:1 icons/borders).
4. **No per-widget `Brightness.dark` checks** in panels/chrome — surfaces come
   from the theme's role tokens (§4.3), and dark mode gets real layering.
5. **The §U6 defect list is empty**, verified by regenerated screenshots.
6. Nothing about the *canvas interaction model* changes. This is chrome.

## 3. The plan

Five stages. Each is independently shippable and each earns the next; the
order is deliberate — tokens before themes before surfaces, because every
later stage spends the vocabulary the earlier one defines.

**Status: stages 1–5 built 2026-08-05**, with two findings withdrawn or
deferred and recorded as such (§U6 items 3 and 6). §7 of this document is
unchanged; the audit results are in §6.

| # | Stage | Size | What it delivers |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Tokens** (`lib/theme/tokens.dart`) | **S** | The §4 decisions as code: type ramp, icon sizes, radii, spacing, alphas, motion durations, surface roles (as a `ThemeExtension`). No visual change yet — the vocabulary. |
| 2 | **Theme completion** | **M** | Fill `onoteTheme()` with component themes: text theme from the ramp; buttons (28/32px, `radius-md`, no stadium); menus (36px rows, elevation-1); dialogs (`radius-xl`=12, plain surface); date/time pickers; snackbar → desktop toast (≤440px, floating, bottom-left); checkbox (15px, radius 3); `NoSplash` + hover/pressed tints; 2px `ink` focus ring. **Biggest visual win per line of code** — 52 snackbars and every dialog snap into the language at once. |
| 3 | **Type & colour migration** | **M** | Sweep `lib/ui`: every `fontSize:`/`size:`/radius/alpha literal → token (the mechanical part); metadata text `graphite400`→`graphite500` (AA); `brass700` for accent text on light; panel headers to the shared style. Screenshot before/after each file. |
| 4 | **Chrome architecture** | **M–L** | One `SidePanel` scaffold (320px, shared header anatomy, one open at a time — the §4.4 policy) adopted by study/planner/tags/links/toc; surface roles applied (canvas vs chrome vs raised, both modes — dark gets layering); shell drops doubled dividers; breadcrumb row folded away; command bar right-cluster grouped, disabled-wall softened (§4.5); status bar priority layout (no self-truncation). |
| 5 | **Defect burn-down & polish** | **M** | §U6 items: reserve the title band (push/clamp blocks below it, or make the title a real layout row); tag gutter into reserved hanging space (no text shift); planner/rollup titles strip Markdown; navigator default widths & truncation; study empty state loses the skeleton bars; motion pass (§ guide 10: 80–220ms fades on menus/panels, reduce-motion honoured). |

**What this plan deliberately does not include:** a rebrand (palette and
wordmark stay), an icon-set swap (Material outline stays for now; Lucide
remains a recorded aspiration — swapping is a *content* change the token
layer will make cheap later), any ribbon/toolbar rethink beyond grouping, and
any change to canvas interaction (§8 of the guide is untouched).

## 4. Decisions taken now (normative — mirrored in Style Guide v0.4)

These are the values the stages implement. They are recorded here once so
review of this plan is review of the system; the style guide carries the same
numbers as the durable reference.

### 4.1 UI type ramp (integer px only)

| Token | Size/height | Weight | Replaces | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ui-title` | 15/20 | 600 | 15, 14 | dialog titles |
| `ui` | 13/18 | 400 | 12.5, 13, 13.5 | default UI text: rows, menus, inputs |
| `ui-strong` | 13/18 | 600 | — | emphasis within `ui` |
| `ui-sm` | 12/16 | 400 | 11.5, 12 | secondary text, subtitles, tooltips |
| `caption` | 11/14 | 400 | 10.5, 11 | metadata, hints, badges, status bar |
| `overline` | 11/14 | 700, +0.5, caps | 10.5 caps | panel titles & list group labels |

9, 9.5 and 10px die (badge counts move to 11); the editor's content scale
(16px body, Markdown headings) is separate and unchanged. Net effect: most of
the UI moves *up* ~1px and onto a rhythm.

### 4.2 Icons, radii, spacing, alphas

- **Icons: 16 / 18 / 20.** 16 default (rows, panel headers), 18 command bar,
  20 rail/empty states. The 12–15px micro-icons move to 16; 17/19 snap to 18.
- **Radii: 4 / 6 / 8 / 12.** 4 inputs+checkboxes+chips · 6 buttons+menu items
  +small controls · 8 menus+popovers+cards · 12 dialogs. Everything else
  (1.5, 2, 3, 5, 7, 10, 11, 14, 16) migrates; `99` stays for pills only.
- **Spacing: the guide's 4-pt scale**, now enforced: control padding 8×12,
  panel padding 12, section rhythm 16 in panels / 24 on pages.
- **Named alphas:** `hover .05` · `selected .10` · `selected-strong .14` ·
  `drag .18` · borders on tint .35. The twelve ad-hoc values collapse into
  these five.

### 4.3 Surface roles (the dark-mode fix)

| Role | Light | Dark | Regions |
|---|---|---|---|
| `canvas` | `paper0` | `night0` | the page itself |
| `chrome` | `paper50` | `night50` | command bar, status bar, side panels |
| `chrome-2` | `paper100` | `night100` | navigator sections column, insets, wells |
| `raised` | `paper0` | `night100` | menus, dialogs, popovers (+ elevation-1/2) |
| `border` | `paper200` | `night200` | all hairlines — panels stop drawing their own |

Rule: **widgets never check `Brightness.dark`**; they name a role. The canvas
is the only `canvas`-role surface, which restores the "page is the hero"
principle in dark mode — chrome sits a step above black, the page sits on it.

### 4.4 One panel slot

Right-hand panels (study, planner, tags, links, outline) share **one 320px
slot, one open at a time** — opening one closes another; the command-bar
toggles become a segmented group. Rationale: five panels at four widths can
currently stack to 1,360px; no workflow was found that needs two panels at
once, and OneNote's task-pane model (one at a time) is the familiar shape.
*(If a real two-panel workflow emerges — study + tags while revising — the
slot gains a pin, not the Row a sixth width.)*

### 4.5 The command bar's first impression

Keep §7a.2's "disabled ≠ hidden" principle, lose the wall: formatting controls
collapse to their group heads when no editor has focus (B/I/U + one "…" per
group rather than ~20 grey glyphs), the hint line stays, and the right cluster
groups into: panel switcher (segmented) · find · share/export. Full breadth
returns the moment a text box has focus — the principle holds, the first
paint doesn't read as broken.

### 4.6 Toasts

SnackBars become desktop toasts: floating, max-width 440, bottom-left (clear
of the panel slot), `radius-8`, `raised` surface, `ui-sm` text, with the
Undo-in-toast pattern preserved. One theme change covers all 52 sites; sites
that pass `SnackBarAction` keep working untouched.

## 5. Risks and brakes

- **Scope creep into redesign.** The brake: the five principles and the
  palette are fixed inputs; any proposal that changes *what* the UI is (new
  navigation model, new toolbar concept) is out — this plan changes how the
  existing UI is *finished*.
- **A thousand tiny diffs.** Stage 3 is mechanical and huge; it must land as
  per-file commits with before/after screenshots, not one 4,000-line diff.
- **Golden-test fragility.** Screenshots are review artifacts, never CI
  assertions (fonts differ per machine) — `test/goldens/` stays gitignored.
- **The `Panel` refactor breaking behaviour.** Panels carry real logic
  (study sessions, planner menus); stage 4 moves *chrome*, not state — the
  existing widget tests (planner 10, study 5, dialogs 6) are the harness.

## 6. Verification

### Results, 2026-08-05

The §2 exit criteria, re-measured after the build:

| Criterion | Before | After | |
|---|---|---|---|
| Distinct font sizes in `lib/ui` | 17 | **5** (11 · 12 · 13 · 15 · 22) | ✅ target ≤6 |
| Distinct icon sizes | 11 | **3** (16 · 18 · 20, plus 32 for empty-state art) | ✅ target 3 |
| Distinct corner radii | 13 | **4** (4 · 6 · 8 · 12) + the pill | ✅ target 4 |
| Per-widget `Brightness.dark` branches | 16 files | **4 sites**, all per-mode *alpha* tuning — the one sanctioned reason (§3.7) | ✅ |
| Text/surface pairings failing AA | `graphite400` everywhere (2.80:1) | **none** — `textSecondary` is 5.06:1 light, 8.23:1 dark | ✅ |
| Stock-M3 shapes visible | snackbars, buttons, dialogs, pickers, menus | **none** — all themed | ✅ |

Two bugs that had shipped were found by the work rather than by the review, and
neither was visible to any state test:

- **Every button label in the app rendered in Roboto, not Inter.**
  `ThemeData.fontFamily` only reaches the text theme Flutter derives from
  typography, and `ButtonStyle.textStyle` overrides the text theme besides. So
  the app bundled a font to look identical on every OS and then set every
  button in a different face from the text beside it.
- **Theming inputs put an outline round the canvas editor.**
  `InputDecorationTheme.enabledBorder` overrides a decoration's own `border`,
  so every field that said `InputBorder.none` grew an outline and 8px of width.
  `edit_view_metrics_test` caught it within a minute.



- The screenshot harness (`app/test/ui_screenshots_test.dart`,
  `ONOTE_SCREENSHOTS=1 … --update-goldens`) is the review instrument: every
  stage regenerates the set and eyeballs it; §U6 closes only on screenshots.
- The §U2 **token audit greps** and the §U4 **contrast matrix** are re-run at
  the end of stages 3 and 5; their targets are the exit criteria in §2.
- `flutter analyze` gains nothing new; the full suite stays green per stage.

## 7. Open questions (deliberately few)

1. **Window chrome.** A custom title bar (merging the breadcrumb into it, à la
   modern desktop apps) is the one *structural* idea this review parked —
   attractive, but platform-fiddly (Windows snap, Linux WMs). Not in v0.6
   unless the owner pulls it in.
2. **Lucide icons.** The token layer makes a later swap cheap; decide after
   the revamp lands, on look alone.
3. **Print dialog.** The OS dialog is the OS's; what we can own is a styled
   in-app preview step before it. Parked pending user testing of P13.
