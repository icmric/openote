Design philosophy

Five principles

Openote is a tool for thinking. The interface should feel like good stationery: never loud, never in the way. Five principles govern every decision.

1
The page is the hero. Everything that isn't the user's content is chrome, and chrome recedes.
2
Calm over clutter. The opposite of OneNote's ribbon. Progressive disclosure; white space is a feature.
3
Interpret, don't interrupt. Formatting appears where you make it — no modal preview panes.
4
Native in spirit, consistent in soul. Honor each platform while keeping one Openote character.
5
Accessible by default. Contrast, keyboard paths, reduced motion and legible type are requirements, not settings.
The idea

Ink, on an open page

The brand concept is ink — the fountain-pen line, the mathematician's margin note, the hand-drawn diagram — set on an open page. It ties together handwriting, math, and the freeform canvas, and the one thing that defines Openote: openness. Two motifs recur — the ink stroke and the open corner.

Personality

The unpretentious craftsperson

Competent, calm, a little opinionated about openness, generous with control. Not a hype startup, not a corporate suite — a well-made open-source tool by people who care, expressed with quiet confidence.

Tagline: "Your notes. Your format. Every platform."

Colour

A palette of ink & brass on paper

Built around two brand hues — Ink (deep indigo, primary) and Brass (warm amber, accent) — on warm-neutral "paper" grays. Chosen to be distinct from OneNote's magenta-purple while staying in the considered, ink-on-paper world. Every pairing is designed to meet WCAG 2.1 AA.

Canvas interaction

The canvas is the product

  • Tools: select/move, text (click empty space to create), pen · highlighter · eraser, lasso (later)
  • Pan: space-drag / two-finger / scrollbars both axes. Zoom: Ctrl-scroll / pinch, centred on cursor
  • Placement: one-tap Free ↔ Snap-to-grid; hold a modifier to invert for one action; alignment guides in Ink 400
  • Parity: every core action works with mouse, trackpad, touch and pen
Accessibility

AA by default

  • All text & UI meet WCAG 2.1 AA; meaning never encoded in colour alone
  • Every action keyboard-reachable; visible focus ring everywhere; no traps
  • Canvas blocks expose type + content to assistive tech (a first-class engineering task)
  • Respect OS text-scaling & "reduce motion"; touch targets ≥ 44px
Voice & tone. Clear, warm, unpretentious — and precise about openness. Errors explain what happened and what to do next, calmly: "Couldn't reach your sync server — your notes are safe on this device. Retry?" We describe competitors accurately: OneNote's format isn't "completely closed," it's "documented but impractical to use elsewhere." Accuracy is part of the brand.

Do

Let the canvas breathe · one Ink primary per view · pair colour with icon/text · animate with purpose · write plainly · meet AA · keep tools contextual.

Don't

Rebuild the cluttered ribbon · make Brass dominant · rely on colour alone · add gratuitous shadows · use modals where inline undo works · overstate claims.
